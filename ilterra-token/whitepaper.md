## Project overview

ILTERRA (ILT) is a fungible Jetton on TON Mainnet intended to connect ILTERRA software products with potential loyalty benefits and participation in decisions about the token ecosystem. The token has been issued. **Discounts, holder voting and product-specific asset rights are not currently active or guaranteed.** This document describes the current project and the framework being considered; it does not announce a public token sale or admission to trading.

## Token and technology

The official Jetton master contract is `EQALRGN8_jJg1dydiWckdiOpTXyUBi0tjBH71QpC6BEHkFLu`. The token name is ILTERRA, its symbol is ILT, and it uses 9 decimal places. The initial mint was **10,000,000 ILT**. The contract currently has an administrator and permits additional minting, so the initial issuance is not a supply cap. See [Token Information](/ilterra-token/token-information/) for the live explorer links and instructions for identifying the authentic token.

TON Jettons use a master contract and separate wallet contracts for holders. A holder's Jetton wallet address is not the same as the master contract address. Names, tickers and logos can be copied by unrelated tokens; the complete master address is the essential identifier.

## Ecosystem plans

ILTERRA is considering loyalty benefits or software subscription discounts for eligible ILT holders. No product currently has a published ILT entitlement. The [Loyalty & Discount Policy](/ilterra-token/loyalty-discount-policy/) explains which eligibility and calculation rules still need to be specified.

The project is also considering token-holder voting on defined decisions, including possible additional issuance. The [Governance Policy](/ilterra-token/governance-policy/) records the proposed framework and its limits. It is not yet operating, and a vote is not enforced by the deployed contract. The [Product & Asset Registry](/ilterra-token/product-asset-registry/) currently lists no committed product or asset rights.

## Issuance and allocation

The [Tokenomics & Allocation](/ilterra-token/tokenomics-allocation/) document records a proposed division of the initial issuance: 30% for the founder, approximately 30% for possible future distribution or sale, and the remaining 40% for ecosystem, liquidity, development and reserves. These are planning figures, not a record of completed transfers, an adopted lock schedule or an offer for sale. Treasury custody, vesting and conditions for future minting require further decisions.

## Holder position and material risks

ILT does not automatically provide equity, dividends, repayment, revenue, ownership of ILTERRA or a right to a product or asset. Digital assets carry network, smart-contract, custody, irreversible-transaction, administrator, dilution, market and liquidity risks. The [Token Terms & Risk Disclosure](/ilterra-token/token-terms-risk-disclosure/) describes these matters in more detail. The value of ILT, if any, can fall to zero.
