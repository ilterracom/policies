## Current status

ILTERRA intends to explore loyalty benefits and software subscription discounts for eligible ILT holders. **No discount, subscription entitlement or product access is currently guaranteed merely by holding ILT.** A participating product will be identified only after its benefit and eligibility rules are approved and made available to users.

FinCast is a possible first candidate, but it is not a confirmed participant and its current published payment terms do not include an ILT discount.

## How a future benefit would be defined

For each participating product, ILTERRA would publish the eligible plans, the benefit offered, who qualifies, when eligibility is measured and how a user claims it. Where wallet ownership needs to be verified, the product may ask the user to prove control of a wallet, for example by signing a message; merely supplying a wallet address would not prove control.

The program rules would also need to specify whether benefits apply to a new purchase, a renewal or both; how they interact with app-store billing; how token balances are measured; any tiers, formula or maximum discount; and exclusions, transfers, refunds and abuse-prevention measures. Applicable consumer and tax rules would also be addressed.

No discount percentage, balance threshold or calculation formula has been approved. A token purchase should not be made in reliance on a future discount.
