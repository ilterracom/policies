## 1. About the Service

MIDI Converter is an online tool that allows users to upload MIDI files and convert them into a standardized MIDI format with a unified tempo across the entire composition. The Service is designed to help musicians and producers prepare MIDI files that are fully compatible with digital audio workstations such as Logic Pro and other DAW software.

Service address: [https://midi.ilterra.com](https://midi.ilterra.com)

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Uploaded MIDI Files

When using MIDI Converter, users may upload MIDI files for conversion.

- Uploaded MIDI files are used solely for the purpose of file processing and conversion.
- Files are processed automatically by the system.
- We do not analyze, listen to, or review musical content.
- Uploaded files are not shared with third parties.

Uploaded files may be temporarily stored on our servers for processing and are automatically deleted after the conversion process is completed.
