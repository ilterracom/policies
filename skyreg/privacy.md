## 1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the “Policy”) establishes the procedure for the processing and use of personal and other data of users of the “SkyReg” application, developed by ILTERRA and distributed on iOS and iPadOS operating systems.

## 2. Collection and Use of Data

2.1. The “SkyReg” app is designed with utmost attention to the protection of your data. User data is encrypted with a key that the user generates in an implicit form. We, as the developer, do not have access to this data and cannot decrypt it.

2.2. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data or information stored within the app with third parties.

2.3. Exceptions might include various services integrated by Apple into the app’s SDK and operating system, as well as advertising services integrated into the app, if they exist.

## 3. Data Security Responsibility

3.1. We do everything possible to ensure the security of your data within the framework of the application. However, the security of data stored on Apple devices and in iCloud is the responsibility of Apple as the hardware manufacturer and the cloud storage service provider.

3.2. Users also bear responsibility for the security of their data, especially if they grant access to the application’s data storage file system.

## 4. Third Parties

4.1. Our services may include third-party services, such as advertising or analytics services. These services might collect and transmit information, which can include but is not limited to unique device identifiers, IP addresses, and locations.

## 5. Changes to the Privacy Policy

5.1. We may periodically update this Policy. Whenever any changes are made, we will publish the updated version on the developer’s official website [https://ilterra.com](https://ilterra.com).

## 6. Feedback

6.1. If you have questions or suggestions regarding this Policy, please contact us through our official website [https://ilterra.com](https://ilterra.com).
